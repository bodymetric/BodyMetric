# Implementation Plan: Remove "Plan Another Week" Button

**Branch**: `024-remove-plan-another-week` | **Date**: 2026-05-31 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)
**Input**: Remove the "Plan another week" button (and its `onRestart` callback) from `PlanSavedView`.

## Summary

`PlanSavedView` currently has two action buttons: "Back to home" (calls `onHome()`) and "Plan another week" (calls `onRestart()`). The "Plan another week" button is being removed. The fix is two files: remove the button + callback property from `PlanSavedView`, and drop the `onRestart:` argument from its single call site in `NewPlanWizardView`.
